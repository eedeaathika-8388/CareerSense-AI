Intermediate / Grammar

Grammar Essentials

Strengthen the grammar needed for professional communication.

Tenses

Present, Past and Future.

Example: I study.
I studied.
I will study.

Articles

A, An and The.

Example: I saw a dog.
She is an engineer.

Prepositions

in, on, at, from, to, with.

Subject-Verb Agreement

He works.
They work.

Active / Passive Voice

Active: She wrote the letter.
Passive: The letter was written by her.

Modals

can, could, may, might, should, must.
